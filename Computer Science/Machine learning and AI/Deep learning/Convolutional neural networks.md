 Convolutional neural networks, abbreviated to ConvNets, are designed to process data stored in the form of multiple arrays (e.g. colour image data composed of three 2D arrays containing pixel intensities and colour channels.) 

The role of the convolutional layer within the network is to detect local conjunctions of features from the previous layer. A pooling layer then merges semantically similar features into one.
