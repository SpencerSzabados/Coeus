Hierarchical diffusion models (in [[Generative models|generative modeling]]) are a type of [[Diffusion models|diffusion model]] that attempt to learn the transitionary conditional densities between denoting steps on mixed data representations which are then combined to generate the final output. This differs from how "standard" diffusion models attempt to learn the transitionary kernels over all the data.
