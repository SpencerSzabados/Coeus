In 3D computer graphics, Phong shading (or Phong interpolation or normal-vector interpolation shading) is a technique for surface shading based on normal-vector interpolation. See [[@Phong_1975]].

![[435px-Phong-shading-sample_(cropped).jpg]]

The Phong's interpolation method improves upon the Gouraud shading, particularly when applied to a reflection model with small specular highlights, e.g., the Phong reflection model.
