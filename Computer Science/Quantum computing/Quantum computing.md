# Overview of underlying quantum mechanics models
Quantum mechanics is an axiomatic theory used in the development of physical theories, providing a conceptual connection between mathematical concepts and the physical.

**Definition: (qubit)** The minimum quantum state is represented by a vector in a bi-directional complex vector space, called a _qubit_, expressing a linear combination of the two possible sates or 0 or 1. Specifically $$\ket{\psi} = \alpha \ket{0} + \beta \ket{1},$$ where $\alpha, \beta \in \mathbb{C}$ satisfying $|\alpha|^2 + |\beta|=1$.  This can be equivalently written as $$\ket{\psi} = cos\left(\frac{\theta}{2}\right)\ket{0} + e^{i\phi}sin\left(\frac{\theta}{2}\right)\ket{1}$$ for coordinates $(\theta, \phi)$ on the unit sphere.
