Our understanding of generalization of deep networks is still not well fleshed out. Few properties that are understood to judge linear model [[Generalization]] are known to carry over (directly) to deep networks.


# Generalization theories 
## Algorithmic stability of deep networks
It is suspected that a weaker stability argument, opposed to those involving [[Generalization#Uniform algorithm stability|uniform statbility]], that is sensitive to the data used holds for deep networks, based on empirical testing measuring the Euclidean distance between parameters of two identical models trained on corrupted data sets (differing in one example or more).


## Capacity of deep networks
People have tried to apply [[Generalization#VC-dimension|vc-dimention]] or [[Generalization#Rademacher complexity|rademacher complexity]] to neural networks.
