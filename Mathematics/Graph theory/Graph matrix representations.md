**Definition: (Adjacency matrix)** Let $G=(V,E)$ be a graph with $V=\{v_1,v_2,\dots,v_n\}$. The _adjacency [[Matrix]]_ of $G$ is the $n$-by-$n$ matrix $A$ with entries $$a_{ij} = \begin{cases}1 & \text{ if } v_iv_j\in E\\ 0 & \text{ otherwise}.\end{cases}$$

**Definition: (Degree sequence)** A sequence of non-negative integers if graphical if it is the degree sequence of some graph.
