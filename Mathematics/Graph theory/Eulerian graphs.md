**Definition: (Euler trail)** Let $G$ be a loopless multigraph. A non-closed walk that traverses every edges of $G$ exactly once is called an _Euler_ trail of $G$.

**Definition:** Let $G$ be a loopless multigraph. $G$ is Eulerian if it contains a Euler circuit.

**Theorem: (Euler's formula)** Suppose that $G=(V,E)$ is a planar embedding with $|V|$ vertices, $|E|$ edges, and $|F|$ faces. Then $|V|-|E|+|F|=2$.
