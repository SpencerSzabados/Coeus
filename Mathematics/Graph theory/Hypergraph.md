**Definition: (Hypergraph or set system)** A _set system or Hypergraph_ is an ordered pair $(V,\mathcal{F})$, where $V$ is a set of elements and $\mathcal{F}$ is a family of subsets of $V$. The elements of $V$ are referred to as the _vertices_ of the hypergraph and the elements of $\mathcal{F}$ the _edges_, just as in simple graphs. (p21)[[@Bondy_2008]].

When $\mathcal{F}$ consists of pairs of elements of $V$, then $(V,\mathcal{F})$ is a simple graph.

**Definition: ($k$-uniform hypergraph)** A hypergraph $H=(V,\mathcal{F})$ is called $k$-uniform, just as analogously defined for simple graphs, if each edge $e\in\mathcal{F}$ is a $k$-set; that is, $|e|=k$.
