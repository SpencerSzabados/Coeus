Phase space, as it is called in [[@Arnold_1992]] (called by others direction fields or slop fields), are plots of the curvature of a differential equation evaluated at test points in the domain akin to the idea of level sets. These plots allow you to know qualitatively how the differential equation will behave given an initial starting point by tracing the points trajectory through test points.
