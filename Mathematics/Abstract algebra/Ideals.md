**Lemma: (Ideal test)** If $R$ is a ring with identity and $I$ is an ideal such that $1\in I$, then $I=R$. 
_Proof:_ We have $I\subset R$ by definition, and as a consequence of $1\in I$ for any $r\in R$ we have $r=r\cdot r = 1\cdot r\in I$ which gives $R\subset I$.

**Definition: (Prime ideal)** A proper ideal $P$ in a commutative [[Rings|ring]] $R$ is called a 
prime ideal if $ab\in P\Rightarrow a\in P\text{ and }b\in P$.

**Theorem: (Maximal ideal)** Let $R$ be a commutative [[Rings|ring]] with identity and $M$ be a idea of $R$. Then $M$ is a maximal ideal if and only if $R/M$ is a [[Fields|field]].
