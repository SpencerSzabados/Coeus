
**Lemma: (Polynomial commutable ring)** If $R$ is a commutable [[Rings|ring]] with identity, then $R[x]$ is a polynomial commutative ring with identity. 


**Theorem: (Underlying ring reduction)** Let $R$ be a commutative [[Rings|ring]] with identity and $r\in R$. There exists a ring homomorphism $\alpha_r:R[x]\to R$ defined as $\alpha_r(p(x)) = P(r)$.
