**Definition: (Group)** A group is a set $G$ together with a law of composition $\cdot(a,b)\to a\cdot b$, a closed binary operation $\cdot:G\times G\to G$, that satisfies for all $a,b\in G$:
 1) $(a\cdot b)\cdot c = a\cdot (b\cdot c)$
 2) There exists an element $e\in G$, called the identity, where $e\cdot a=a\cdot e=a$
 3) There exists an element $a^{-1}\in G$ such that $a\cdot a^{-1}=a^{-1}\cdot a = e$.

Note, we do not yet know if $a^{-1}$ equals its common algebraic counter part (negative or reciprocal), this values will depend on the structure of the group.

**Definition: (Group homomorphism)** Let $(G,\cdot )$ and $(H,\circ)$ be two groups. The map $\alpha:G\to H$ such that $\alpha(g_1\cdot g_2)=\alpha(g_1)\circ\alpha(g_2)$ for all $g_1,g_2\in G$ is called a _group homomorphism_. ^b4ee53
