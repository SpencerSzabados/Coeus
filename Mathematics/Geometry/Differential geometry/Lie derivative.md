The Lie derivative of a [[Vector fields|vector field]] (or more generally, tensors -- referring to [[Multilinear maps]] defined over [[Vector space]]) represents the differential (rate of change) of said vector field with respect to another vector field defined over a smooth [[Differential manifolds|manifold]] (or just in Euclidean space); that is, it allows you to compute the derivative of a vector field and observe its change as the datum (reference point) flows through another vector field.
