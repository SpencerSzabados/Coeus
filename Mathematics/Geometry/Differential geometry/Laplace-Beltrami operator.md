The laplace-Beltrami operator originates from attempting to smoothly, under some sense of smoothness akin to the heat equation, interpolating between boundary (and or initial conditions) in a region (e.g., [[Subspace manifolds]], [[Combinatorial manifolds#^48deab|Simplical Complexes]], etc). It is a generalization of the [[Laplacian operator]] to [[Differential manifolds]].
