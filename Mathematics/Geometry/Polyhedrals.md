Polyhedral theory is important within the context of [[Semidefinite programming]] and [[Semidefinite optimization]].

# Overview
**Definition: (Affine independence)** A set of vectors $v^{(1)},v^{(2)},\dots, v^{(k)}\in\mathbb{R}^d$ are said to be _affine independent_ if $[v^{(1)}|1],\dots,[v^{(k)}|1]$ are linearly independent in $\mathbb{R}^{d+1}$; equivalently these vectors are affine independent if the set of $k-1$ vectors $(v^{(2)}-v^{(1)}),\dots,(v^{(k)}-v^{(1)})$ are linearly independent.

Note that, at most $d+1$ points can be affinely independent in $\mathbb{R}^d$.

**Definition: (Polyhedral dimension)** let $P$ be a polyhedron in $\mathbb{R}^d$. The dimension of $P$ is equal to the maximum number of affinely intendent points in $P$ minus one. Thus, $dim(P)\leq d$.
