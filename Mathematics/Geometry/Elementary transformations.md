**Definition: (Similarity)** Two geometric objects are said to be similar if one can be obtained from the other by a _similarity transformation_ consisting of a finite sequence of dilations and rigid motions (e.g., a uniform scaling, translation, rotation, or reflection), see [[@Modenov_1965]].

**Definition: (Shear)** An affine transformation in the plane where each point of an object is displaced horizontally by a distance proportional to it's ordinate ($y$-coordinate), is called a _shear transformation_. Functionally, shear transformations take the form $f:\mathbb{R}^2\to\mathbb{R}^2$ with $$f(S)=\{(x+\alpha y,y) : (x,y)\in S\}$$ for a geometric object $S$, here represented as a collection of points, and a positive scalar $\alpha$.
