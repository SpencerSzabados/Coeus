Sampling based or more properly [[Stochastic Process|stochastic]] optimization methods make use of randomization to help solve optimization problems that might otherwise be computationally infeasible (e.g., to expensive to compute an exact solution).

**Examples of Sampling based methods:**
  + [[Simulated annealing process]]
  + [[Gradient decent#Stochastic gradient descent|Stochastic gradient descent]]

# Convergence rates
Convergence towards optimal values is characterized by the [[Monte Carlo simulation#^549186|mixing time]] of the process and the time needed for the sampling process to converge to the estimated minimizer for the limiting distribution.
