**Definition: (Banach space)** A Banach space, denoted $(B,\|\cdot\|)$, is a normed [[Vector space]] that is complete (meaning every Cauchy sequence converges wrt to the norm). 

Every Banach space is a [[Metric spaces|metric space]], as every normed vector space is a metric space, but the converse is not always true. Moreover, there exists norms that are not induced by metrics and within metrics spaces alone there is no set operations for adding and multiplying elements.
