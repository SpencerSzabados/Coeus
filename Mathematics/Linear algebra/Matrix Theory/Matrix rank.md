The rank of a matrix $A$ is the dimension of the [[Vector space]] generated by the [[Vector space#^318dc4|span]] of the matrices columns.

**Definition: (Matrix rank)** Let $A$ be a $m\times n$ matrix. The (column) rank of $A$ is the maximal number of linearly independent column vectors present within $A$; that is, the rank of $A$ is the dimension of the column space (or span of column vectors) of $A$. Likewise, the row rank of $A$ is he dimension of the row space of $A$, or the rank of $A^\intercal$. ^6a57b5
