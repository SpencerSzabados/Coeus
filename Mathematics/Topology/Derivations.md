A derivation is a function (operator) on [[Algebras]] that generalized aspects of the standard derivative operator. 

**Definition: (Derivation)** A _derivation_ of an [[Algebras|algebra]] $A$ over a ring $R$, which is defined over a [[Fields|field]] $\mathbb{F}$, a $k$-[[Multilinear maps|linear map]] $D:A\to A$ such that: $D(ab)=aD(b)+D(a)b$.

Eg) The Lie derivative wrt a [[Vector fields|vector field]] is an $R$-[[Algebras#^ecd19a|algebra]] is an $R$-derivation on the algebra of differentiable functions defined over the associated differentiable [[Combinatorial manifolds|manifold]].
