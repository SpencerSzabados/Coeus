
**Definition: (Topological embedding)** An injective continuous map that is a [[homeomorphism]] onto its range, under the subspace topology, is called a topological embedding; that is, a map $f:X\to Y$ between two [[Topological spaces|topological spaces]] is called a topological embedding, denoted $f: X\hookrightarrow Y$, if the function $F: X\to f(X)$, which is constructed from $f$ by restricting its codomain to be exactly equal to its range, is a homeomorphism with respect to the subspace topology applied to $f(X)$. See [StackExchange-Koda](https://math.stackexchange.com/questions/2923537/definition-of-topological-embedding).
