**Definition: (Measure dominance)** A measure $v$ is said to be dominated by the measure $\mu$, or equivalently $b$ is absolutely continuous with respect to the measure $\mu$, if for every measure able set $A$, $\mu(A)=0$ implies $v(A)=0$. ^d5a421
