Boltzmann machines are graphs (e.g., [[Artificial neural networks]]) that have some "energy" (a distribution of values) spread across the nodes that follows a stochastic process at each node.

These networks can be used to solve two primary problems: Optimization search problems and consequently learning problems that can be formulated into an approximate optimization problem.
