 In the most general sense, stochastic processes model the evolution of [[Fundamentals of probability theory#^4b13e7|events]] through time that involve randomness; e.g., [[Diffusion models]].

# Overview
**Definition: (Stochastic process)** A _stochastic process_ is a family of [[Random variables#^5ceb15|random variables]] $\{X_t\}$, where $t$ is a parameter running over a suitable index set $T$. [[@Taylor_1998]]

Stochastic processes are distinguished by their _state space_, or the range of possible values for the random variables $X_t$, by their index sets $T$, and by the various dependence relations among the random variables.
